DefenSYS Workspace Redesign Concepts

Proposed UI/UX improvements for Adviser and Instructor workflows.

1. Adviser Workspace — Streamlined Team Dossier

Consolidates team header, member avatars, and defense stage stepper into a single compact bar (~75px). Adds top-level action triage chips (Needs Review, Ready for Defense) and tab counters.

2. Instructor Workspace — Cohort Submissions & Compliance Matrix

Designed for section-wide oversight across 15–40+ teams. Clean data table showing team progress, deliverable completion, and direct inline review actions.
